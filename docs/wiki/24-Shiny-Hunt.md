# 找闪光 ✨ · Shiny Hunt ✨

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「✨ 找闪光」，或者右键菜单「✨ 找闪光…」。
- 格子里全是出场的宝可梦，其中**一只是闪光**：颜色和别的稍有不同。**点**它！
- 找对了闪光的那只会露出开心的表情；点错了，被点的那只和闪光的那只都会难过。
- 找到一只就换一盘：格子越来越多（最多 6×6），颜色也越来越接近。点错了会给你看闪光在哪，然后换一盘。
- 限时 **30 秒**。找到 3 只 1 🍓，8 只 2 🍓，14 只 3 🍓，20 只以上 4 🍓；还有经验和心情。会记下最好成绩。
- **谁出场**：每局从队伍里随机挑一只已经孵化、没在外出的宝可梦出场，窗口里会写“这局由…出场”。出场的它拿到这局的全部经验；如果它不是当前伙伴，伙伴也会得到约四分之一的经验。心情仍然加给当前伙伴。队伍里还没有宝可梦时由皮卡丘出场。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。不用联网；出场宝可梦的头像还没下载过时用圆点代替。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "✨ Shiny Hunt", or use the tray menu.
- The grid is full of the Pokémon playing, and **one of them is a shiny** with slightly different colours. **Click** it!
- Find the shiny and it looks happy; click the wrong one and both it and the shiny look sad.
- Each find brings a new board with more cells (up to 6×6) and closer colours. A wrong click shows where the shiny
  was, then a new board comes.
- **30 seconds**. 3 found = 1 🍓, 8 = 2 🍓, 14 = 3 🍓, 20 or more = 4 🍓, plus XP and mood. Your best is kept.
- **Who plays**: each round, a random team member that has hatched and isn't away on an outing plays; the window
  says "… plays this round". It gets all the round's XP, and when it isn't your partner, your partner gets about a
  quarter of that too. Mood still goes to your partner. Pikachu plays while you have no team yet.
- **6 rounds a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline; until the player's portrait has
  been downloaded the cells show dots.
