# 电光一闪 ⚡ · Quick Attack ⚡

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「⚡ 电光一闪」，或者右键菜单「⚡ 电光一闪…」。
- 出场的宝可梦在草丛里等着。红色的 **“！”** 一出现，就尽快**点一下**画面（或按空格）！
- “！”出现前就点了算**抢跑**，这一次不得分。“！”出现后 1.5 秒还没点也不得分。
- 260 毫秒以内 3 分，380 毫秒以内 2 分，600 毫秒以内 1 分。一局 **5 次**，满分 15 分。
- 3 分 1 🍓，6 分 2 🍓，10 分 3 🍓，13 分以上 4 🍓；还有经验和心情。会记下你最快的反应时间。
- **谁出场**：每局从队伍里随机挑一只已经孵化、没在外出的宝可梦出场，窗口里会写“这局由…出场”。出场的它拿到这局的全部经验；如果它不是当前伙伴，伙伴也会得到约四分之一的经验。心情仍然加给当前伙伴。队伍里还没有宝可梦时由皮卡丘出场。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。出场的宝可梦是会动的像素形象，出手时会攻击、抢跑时会受伤（下载过一次以后不用联网；还没下载时先显示头像）。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "⚡ Quick Attack", or use the tray menu.
- The Pokémon playing waits in the grass. When the red **"!"** flashes, **click** the picture (or press Space) as fast as you can!
- Clicking before the "!" is a **false start** and scores nothing. Not clicking within 1.5 seconds scores nothing too.
- Under 260 ms = 3 points, under 380 ms = 2, under 600 ms = 1. **5 draws** a round, 15 points at most.
- 3 points = 1 🍓, 6 = 2 🍓, 10 = 3 🍓, 13 or more = 4 🍓, plus XP and mood. Your fastest reaction is kept.
- **Who plays**: each round, a random team member that has hatched and isn't away on an outing plays; the window
  says "… plays this round". It gets all the round's XP, and when it isn't your partner, your partner gets about a
  quarter of that too. Mood still goes to your partner. Pikachu plays while you have no team yet.
- **6 rounds a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. The player is its animated sprite: it attacks
  on a good hit and gets hurt on a false start (offline once downloaded; its portrait until then).
