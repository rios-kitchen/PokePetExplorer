# 霹雳电球翻牌 💣 · Voltorb Flip 💣

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「💣 霹雳电球翻牌」，或者右键菜单「💣 霹雳电球翻牌…」。和《心金魂银》游戏城里的一样。
- 5×5 张牌，每张是 **1、2、3** 或者**霹雳电球**。每行右边、每列下面写着这一行（列）的**数字总和**和**电球个数**，靠它们推理。
- **点击**翻牌，**右键**给你觉得是电球的牌做记号。翻到的数字相乘就是金币。
- 把所有的 **2 和 3** 都翻出来就赢：+3 🍓（金币 48 以上 +4 🍓）、经验 +30、心情 +8。翻到电球这局就结束，只有一点经验。会记下一共赢了几局。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。不用联网。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "💣 Voltorb Flip", or use the tray menu. Just like the Game Corner in
  HeartGold and SoulSilver.
- 25 cards, each a **1, 2 or 3** – or a **Voltorb**. Beside each row and under each column: that line's **total** and how
  many **Voltorb** it hides. Work it out from those.
- **Click** to turn a card over, **right-click** to mark one you think is a Voltorb. The cards you turn multiply into coins.
- Turn over every **2 and 3** to win: +3 🍓 (+4 from 48 coins), +30 XP, mood +8. A Voltorb ends the board with a little XP.
  Your total wins are kept.
- **6 boards a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline.
