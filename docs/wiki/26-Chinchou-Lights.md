# 灯笼鱼点灯 💡 · Chinchou Lights 💡

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「💡 灯笼鱼点灯」，或者右键菜单「💡 灯笼鱼点灯…」。
- 夜晚的池塘里有 4×4 只灯笼鱼，有些灯灭了。**点**一只，它和**上下左右**的邻居都会切换亮灭。
- 把**所有灯笼鱼都点亮**就赢了，它们会一起露出开心的表情。上面会显示“标准”步数：这个池塘一定能在这么多步内点亮。
- 不超过标准 4 🍓，标准 + 4 步以内 3 🍓，+ 10 步以内 2 🍓，再多 1 🍓；还有经验和心情。会记下最少步数。
- 小窍门：同一只点两下等于没点，点的先后顺序也不影响结果。
- 每天 **6 个池塘**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 个**，零点重置。灯笼鱼都是会动的像素形象（下载过一次以后不用联网；还没下载时先显示头像）。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "💡 Chinchou Lights", or use the tray menu.
- A night-time pond of 4×4 Chinchou, some with their lights off. **Tap** one and it switches, together with its
  neighbours **up, down, left and right**.
- Light up **every Chinchou** to win – they all put on a happy face. The "par" shown at the top is a number of taps this pond can surely be lit in.
- At par = 4 🍓, within par + 4 = 3 🍓, within par + 10 = 2 🍓, more = 1 🍓, plus XP and mood. Your fewest taps are kept.
- Tip: tapping the same Chinchou twice undoes it, and the order of your taps doesn't matter.
- **6 ponds a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Every Chinchou is its animated sprite (offline once
  downloaded; its portrait until then).
