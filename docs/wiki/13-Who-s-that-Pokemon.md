# 猜猜我是谁 ❓ · Who's That Pokémon? ❓

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「❓ 猜猜我是谁」，或者右键菜单「❓ 猜猜我是谁…」。
- 屏幕上是一只宝可梦的**黑色剪影**（PMD 像素头像），下面有 4 个名字，选一个。选完剪影会揭晓。
- **答对**：+2 🍓，**连续答对第 3、6、9……题**再多 +1 🍓；当前的宝可梦也一起高兴（心情 +5，经验 +15，亲密度也会变高）。
- **答错**：没有奖励，连对次数清零。
- 一半的题目是你在图鉴里见过的宝可梦（见过 5 只以上时），另一半是随机的；刚出过的不会马上再出。
- 每天 **12 题**，任意 1 小时内最多 **4 题**，零点重置。需要网络（头像和名字来自 PMD SpriteCollab 和 PokeAPI）。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "❓ Who's That Pokémon?", or use the tray menu.
- You see a Pokémon's **black silhouette** (its PMD pixel portrait) and 4 names: pick one, and the face is revealed.
- **Right**: +2 🍓, and +1 🍓 more for **every 3rd right answer in a row**; your Pokémon is happy too (mood +5, XP +15,
  and friendship grows).
- **Wrong**: nothing, and the streak starts over.
- Half the questions are Pokémon from your Pokédex (once you've met at least 5), the rest are random; recent answers
  don't come back right away.
- **12 questions a day**, at most **4 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Needs the internet (faces from PMD
  SpriteCollab, names from PokeAPI).
