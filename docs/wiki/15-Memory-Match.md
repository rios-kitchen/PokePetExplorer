# 记忆翻牌 🃏 · Memory Match 🃏

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「🃏 记忆翻牌」，或者右键菜单「🃏 记忆翻牌…」。
- 16 张牌背面朝上，里面是 **8 对宝可梦头像**（优先用你队伍里和图鉴里见过的宝可梦）。每次翻两张：一样就留着，不一样就会翻回去。
- 找齐 8 对就结束，按用的**回合数**给奖励：12 回合以内 +4 🍓，16 回合以内 +3，22 回合以内 +2，更多回合 +1；
  经验 40 起（8 回合最完美），每多一回合少 2，最少 10；心情 +6（亲密度也会变高）。会记下最好成绩。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。第一次需要联网下载头像。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "🃏 Memory Match", or use the tray menu.
- 16 face-down cards hide **8 pairs of Pokémon faces** (Pokémon from your team and Pokédex first). Turn two at a time:
  a pair stays face up, anything else turns back over.
- Once all 8 pairs are found you're rewarded by the **turns** it took: +4 🍓 within 12 turns, +3 within 16, +2 within
  22, +1 after that; 40 XP for a perfect 8 turns, 2 less for every extra turn (at least 10); mood +6 (friendship grows
  too). Your best is kept.
- **6 games a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. The faces are downloaded the first time, so it needs
  the internet then.
