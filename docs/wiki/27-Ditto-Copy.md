# 百变怪照着变 🟣 · Ditto Copy 🟣

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「🟣 百变怪照着变」，或者右键菜单「🟣 百变怪照着变…」。
- 4×4 的格子里会亮出几只**百变怪**，一会儿就消失。记住它们在哪，然后**点出同样的格子**（顺序不要紧）。
- 点够数量就自动判定：全对是“一模一样”；不对的话，**绿框**是漏掉的格子，**红框**是点错的格子。
- 一共 **5 轮**，每轮多一只：3、4、5、6、7 只。
- 照对 1 轮 1 🍓，2 轮 2 🍓，4 轮 3 🍓，5 轮全对 4 🍓；还有经验和心情。会记下最好成绩。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。不用联网；百变怪的头像还没下载过时显示紫色圆团。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "🟣 Ditto Copy", or use the tray menu.
- Some **Ditto** light up on a 4×4 grid for a moment, then disappear. Remember where they were and **tap the same
  cells** (any order).
- Once you have tapped enough cells the copy is checked: a **green ring** marks a cell you missed, a **red ring** a
  wrong tap.
- **5 rounds**, each with one more Ditto: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
- 1 round copied = 1 🍓, 2 = 2 🍓, 4 = 3 🍓, all 5 = 4 🍓, plus XP and mood. Your best is kept.
- **6 games a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline; until Ditto's portrait has been
  downloaded the grid shows purple blobs.
