# 属性相克 ⚔️ · Type Match-up ⚔️

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「⚔️ 属性相克」，或者右键菜单「⚔️ 属性相克…」。
- 某个属性的招式打中某个属性的宝可梦，效果怎么样？从 **效果绝佳 / 一般 / 效果不好 / 没有效果** 里选一个，和原作的说法一样。
- 每局 **10 题**，从第 6 题起宝可梦是**双属性**（倍率相乘，比如 ×4 也算效果绝佳、×¼ 也算效果不好）。
- 每答对 3 题 +1 🍓，10 题全对再 +1（每局最多 4 个）；每题经验 +4；答对 7 题以上心情 +6（亲密度也会变高）。会记下最好成绩。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **2 局**，零点重置。不用联网。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "⚔️ Type Match-up", or use the tray menu.
- An attack of one type hits a Pokémon of another: how well does it work? Pick **super effective / normal / not very
  effective / no effect**, as the games put it.
- **10 questions** a round; from the 6th on the Pokémon has **two types** (the multipliers multiply: ×4 is still super
  effective, ×¼ still not very effective).
- +1 🍓 for every 3 right, +1 more for all 10 (at most 4 a round); +4 XP per right answer; mood +6 from 7 right
  (friendship grows too). Your best is kept.
- **6 rounds a day**, at most **2 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline.
