# 藏在哪个球里 🔴 · Poké Ball Shuffle 🔴

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「🔴 藏在哪个球里」，或者右键菜单「🔴 藏在哪个球里…」。
- 出场的宝可梦会先从一个精灵球里探出头，然后躲进去。精灵球开始换来换去，换完以后点出它在哪个球里。猜对了它会**开心地**跳出来；猜错了它会从真正的球里**难过地**探出头。
- 一共 **5 轮**：前两轮 3 个球，接着 4 个，最后一轮 5 个；换的次数越来越多，速度越来越快。点错一次这局就结束。
- 找到 1 次得 1 🍓，3 次 2 🍓，4 次 3 🍓，5 次全中 4 🍓；还有经验和心情。会记下最好成绩。
- **谁出场**：每局从队伍里随机挑一只已经孵化、没在外出的宝可梦出场，窗口里会写“这局由…出场”。出场的它拿到这局的全部经验；如果它不是当前伙伴，伙伴也会得到约四分之一的经验。心情仍然加给当前伙伴。队伍里还没有宝可梦时由皮卡丘出场。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。不用联网（出场宝可梦的头像下载过就能显示）。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "🔴 Poké Ball Shuffle", or use the tray menu.
- The Pokémon playing peeks out of one Poké Ball and hides inside. The balls get swapped around; when they stop, click the
  one it's in. Find it and it pops out **happy**; pick the wrong one and it peeks out of the right ball looking **sad**.
- **5 rounds**: 3 balls for the first two, then 4, then 5 in the last, with more and quicker swaps each time. One
  wrong ball ends the game.
- 1 found = 1 🍓, 3 = 2 🍓, 4 = 3 🍓, all 5 = 4 🍓, plus XP and mood. Your best is kept.
- **Who plays**: each round, a random team member that has hatched and isn't away on an outing plays; the window
  says "… plays this round". It gets all the round's XP, and when it isn't your partner, your partner gets about a
  quarter of that too. Mood still goes to your partner. Pikachu plays while you have no team yet.
- **6 games a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline (the player's portrait shows once
  it has been downloaded).
