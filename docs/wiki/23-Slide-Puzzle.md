# 拼图 🧩 · Slide Puzzle 🧩

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「🧩 拼图」，或者右键菜单「🧩 拼图…」。
- 出场宝可梦的照片（每局随机换一种表情）被切成 3×3 的 9 块，右下角少了一块，其余的被打乱了。右边的小图是拼好的样子，每块左上角的数字也能帮你认位置。
- **点击**空位旁边的方块，它就滑进空位。把照片拼回原样就完成了，最后一块会自己补上。
- 卡住了可以点「💡 提示」：下一块该滑的方块会发光，你跟着走的话接下来 5 步都会一直指路。每局 **3 次**提示。
- 30 步以内 4 🍓，60 步以内 3 🍓，100 步以内 2 🍓，更多 1 🍓；**一次提示都没用再多 2 🍓**，经验和心情也更多。会记下最少步数。
- **谁出场**：每局从队伍里随机挑一只已经孵化、没在外出的宝可梦出场，窗口里会写“这局由…出场”。出场的它拿到这局的全部经验；如果它不是当前伙伴，伙伴也会得到约四分之一的经验。心情仍然加给当前伙伴。队伍里还没有宝可梦时由皮卡丘出场。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **3 局**，零点重置。不用联网；出场宝可梦的头像还没下载过时用数字方块代替照片。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "🧩 Slide Puzzle", or use the tray menu.
- The player's picture (with a different expression each time) is cut into 9 tiles (3×3), the bottom-right one is missing and the rest are mixed up. The
  small picture on the right shows the goal, and the number in each tile's corner helps too.
- **Click** a tile next to the gap to slide it in. Put the picture back together to finish – the last piece drops in by
  itself.
- Stuck? Click "💡 Hint": the next tile to slide glows, and keeps showing the way for the next 5 moves while you
  follow it. **3 hints** a puzzle.
- 30 moves or fewer = 4 🍓, 60 = 3 🍓, 100 = 2 🍓, more = 1 🍓, plus XP and mood; **solve it without a hint for 2 🍓
  more** (and more XP and mood). Your fewest moves are kept.
- **Who plays**: each round, a random team member that has hatched and isn't away on an outing plays; the window
  says "… plays this round". It gets all the round's XP, and when it isn't your partner, your partner gets about a
  quarter of that too. Mood still goes to your partner. Pikachu plays while you have no team yet.
- **6 puzzles a day**, at most **3 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline; until the player's portrait
  has been downloaded the tiles show numbers instead.
