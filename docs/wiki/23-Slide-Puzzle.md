# 拼图 🧩 · Slide Puzzle 🧩

## 中文

- 在 🧭 外出页的「去玩」里点「🧩 拼图」，或者右键菜单「🧩 拼图…」。
- 伙伴的照片被切成 3×3 的 9 块，右下角少了一块，其余的被打乱了。右边的小图是拼好的样子，每块左上角的数字也能帮你认位置。
- **点击**空位旁边的方块，它就滑进空位。把照片拼回原样就完成了，最后一块会自己补上。
- 30 步以内 4 🍓，60 步以内 3 🍓，100 步以内 2 🍓，更多 1 🍓；还有经验和心情。会记下最少步数。
- 每天 **6 局**，任意 1 小时内最多 **2 局**，零点重置。不用联网；伙伴的头像还没下载过时用数字方块代替照片。

## English

- In the 🧭 Outings tab under "Play outside", click "🧩 Slide Puzzle", or use the tray menu.
- Your partner's picture is cut into 9 tiles (3×3), the bottom-right one is missing and the rest are mixed up. The
  small picture on the right shows the goal, and the number in each tile's corner helps too.
- **Click** a tile next to the gap to slide it in. Put the picture back together to finish – the last piece drops in by
  itself.
- 30 moves or fewer = 4 🍓, 60 = 3 🍓, 100 = 2 🍓, more = 1 🍓, plus XP and mood. Your fewest moves are kept.
- **6 puzzles a day**, at most **2 within any hour**, reset at midnight. Works offline; until your partner's portrait
  has been downloaded the tiles show numbers instead.
