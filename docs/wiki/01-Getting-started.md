# 入门 · Getting started

## 中文

| 操作 | 效果 |
|---|---|
| 第一次打开 | 弹出「大木博士」窗口，从**妙蛙种子、小火龙、杰尼龟**里选一只作为第一只搭档（和原作一样 1/4096 概率是闪光）。右上角的按钮可以切换中文 / English。关掉了也可以从右键菜单「🔴 选择你的第一只宝可梦…」重新打开。三只初始宝可梦的形象已经装在安装包里，第一次打开不联网也能看到。 |
| 单击宝可梦 | 摸摸它：冒爱心、心情上升；每 15 秒最多 +1 经验，每天最多 20 点。 |
| 拖动宝可梦 | 把它放到屏幕任意位置。外出时桌面上的小地图也可以拖动。 |
| 拖着甩出去再松手 | 它会飞出去、在屏幕边缘反弹，落地时有点晕（但很开心）。 |
| 放到屏幕左右边缘 | 它会躲一半在边缘后面偷看；点一下就出来。 |
| 戳不同部位 | 头：摸摸头；肚子：咯咯笑；脚：吱一声。戳太多次它会生气。 |
| 双击宝可梦 | 打开待办清单。 |
| 右键宝可梦 / 托盘图标 | 主菜单：番茄钟、照顾、外出、钓鱼、草丛、日记、切换宝可梦、大小、语言、设置等。 |
| 点头顶的计时胶囊 | 打开番茄钟页面。 |

**控制面板**的分页按用途分组：
- **💼 工作**：番茄钟和待办。
- **📅 今日**、**📔 日记**：每天的签到、任务和日记，放在一起。
- **🐾 宝可梦**（队伍）、**🍓 照顾**（喂食、洗澡、看病、用背包里的东西）、**🛒 商店**（买东西放进背包）、**🧭 外出**（去草丛对战、去钓鱼、外出探险）、**📖 图鉴**（只看收集成果：合影、宝可梦图鉴、钓鱼图鉴、成就、纪念品；每一部分都可以点标题收起或展开，会记住你的选择）。
- **⚙️ 设置**。

## English

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| First launch | Professor Oak's window opens: pick **Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle** as your first partner (1 in 4096 is shiny, as in the games). The button at the top right switches between English and 中文. If you close it, reopen it from the right-click menu "🔴 Choose your first Pokémon…". The three starters' sprites come with the installer, so this works offline. |
| Click the pet | Pat it: hearts, mood up; at most +1 XP every 15 s, 20 a day. |
| Drag the pet | Move it anywhere on screen. While it's away, the little map on the desktop can be dragged too. |
| Drag, swing and let go | It flies, bounces off the screen edges and lands a bit dizzy (and happy). |
| Drop it at the left/right edge | It hides halfway and peeks out; click it to bring it back. |
| Poke different spots | Head: a pat; belly: a giggle; feet: a squeak. Poke too much and it gets annoyed. |
| Double-click | Open the to-do list. |
| Right-click / tray icon | Main menu: timer, care, outings, fishing, grass, diary, switch Pokémon, size, language, settings… |
| Click the timer pill | Open the Pomodoro view. |

The **panel**'s tabs are grouped by what they're for:
- **💼 Work**: Pomodoro timer and to-dos.
- **📅 Today** and **📔 Diary**: the daily sign-in, tasks and diary, side by side.
- **🐾 Pokémon** (team), **🍓 Care** (feed, bathe, treat, use what's in the bag), **🛒 Shop** (buy into the bag),
  **🧭 Outing** (the grass, fishing, expeditions), **📖 Dex** (only what you've collected: photos, Pokédex, fishing
  Pokédex, achievements, souvenirs – click a heading to fold or unfold it; it remembers).
- **⚙️ Settings**.
