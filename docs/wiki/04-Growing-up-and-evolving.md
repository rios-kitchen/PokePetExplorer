# 成长与进化 · Growing up and evolving

## 中文

- **等级**：和原作的「中等偏快」经验曲线一样，升到 Lv n 一共需要 **n³ − 1** 经验（Lv10 = 999，Lv16 = 4095，Lv36 = 46655），**最高 Lv100**。
- 软件开着时宝可梦会**自己慢慢长**：满速每小时 +4 经验。心情不好、生病、饿或脏都会减速（参照 QQ 宠物的成长惩罚），晕倒时不长。
- **进化**：在**原作的进化等级**进化（数据来自 PokeAPI，例如妙蛙种子 Lv16、小火龙 Lv16 / Lv36）。和原作一样，有的要用道具、交换或亲密度：
  - **进化石**（火之石、水之石、雷之石、叶之石、月之石、日之石、光之石、暗之石、觉醒之石、冰之石）：在 🛒 商店买（40 🍓），在 🍓 照顾页对宝可梦使用，马上进化，不看等级。例如皮卡丘用雷之石进化成雷丘，伊布用不同的石头进化成不同的形态。
  - **通信交换进化**（勇基拉、豪力、隆隆石、鬼斯通……）：这里没人可以交换，就用**联系绳**（60 🍓）代替，用法和进化石一样。
  - **亲密度进化**（皮丘、超音蝠、吉利蛋、伊布的太阳伊布 / 月亮伊布……）：亲密度达到 **220** 后升级时进化（最高 255）。亲密度从 70 开始，照顾它、让它开心（喂食、洗澡、番茄钟、摸摸……）就会慢慢变高。
  - 其他方式（特定地点、学会某个招式、某个时间段等）这里在 **Lv30** 进化。
  - 宝可梦页的卡片上能看到亲密度（♥）和它能进化成什么、怎么进化。
  - 道具没有效果时不会消耗。
- **进化还需要时间**：加入队伍**满 7 天**才能第一次进化，上次进化后**满 14 天**才能再进化（用道具也一样）。等级和天数都达到后，宝可梦会先问你，可以选「以后再说」（6 小时后再问）；有分支进化的可以自己选。进化后等级、能力和日记都保留。
- **四项能力**（华丽大赛式）：**帅气、可爱、聪明、强壮**。来自番茄钟、外出、训练课、对战，也会给对战带来小加成。

## English

- **Levels** follow the games' "medium fast" curve: reaching Lv n takes **n³ − 1** XP in total (Lv10 = 999,
  Lv16 = 4095, Lv36 = 46655), **up to Lv100**.
- The pet **grows by itself while the app runs**: +4 XP per hour at full speed, slower when it's sad, sick, hungry or
  dirty (QQ Pet's growth penalties), not at all while fainted.
- **Evolution** happens at **the level from the games** (from PokeAPI: Bulbasaur Lv16, Charmander Lv16 / Lv36 …).
  As in the games, some need an item, a trade or friendship:
  - **Evolution stones** (Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf, Moon, Sun, Shiny, Dusk, Dawn, Ice): buy one in the 🛒 Shop
    (40 🍓) and use it on the Pokémon in the 🍓 Care tab – it evolves right away, at any level. Pikachu becomes Raichu
    with a Thunder Stone; Eevee becomes a different form with each stone.
  - **Trade evolutions** (Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter …): there's no one to trade with, so a **Linking Cord**
    (60 🍓) does it instead, used like a stone.
  - **Friendship evolutions** (Pichu, Zubat's line, Chansey, Eevee into Espeon / Umbreon …): it evolves on a level-up
    once its friendship is **220** (up to 255). Friendship starts at 70 and grows when you take care of it and keep it
    happy (food, baths, Pomodoros, petting …).
  - Other ways (a place, knowing a move, a time of day …) happen at **Lv30** here.
  - The Pokémon tab shows each one's friendship (♥) and what it evolves into, and how.
  - An item that has no effect isn't used up.
- **Evolving also takes time**: at least **7 days** on your team before the first evolution and **14 days** after the
  previous one (with an item, too). Once both the level and the days are there, the pet asks first; "Later" asks again in 6 hours. For
  branching species you pick the form. Level, stats and diary are kept.
- **Four contest stats**: **Cool, Cute, Smart, Tough**. They come from Pomodoros, outings, classes and battles, and give small bonuses in battle.
