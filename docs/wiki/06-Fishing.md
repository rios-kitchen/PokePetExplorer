# 钓鱼 🎣 · Fishing 🎣

## 中文

右键 →「🎣 钓鱼…」，或 🧭 外出页 →「去钓鱼」（旁边会显示今天和这一小时还能钓几次）。

1. **甩竿**：按住鼠标（或空格）蓄力，松开甩出。蓄力条越往上涨得越快，在最远处只停留一瞬间，想甩到最远要掐准时机。甩竿的颜色决定难度：**绿色**（近、简单）、**黄色**（普通）、**红色**（远、难，但能钓到更稀有的）。
2. **等待**：浮漂往下沉时**尽快点击**。反应时间：普通 1.1 秒，非常稀有的只有 0.65 秒；红区更短，绿区更长。
3. **收线**：按住收线。鱼线有**惯性**：按住时张力会越涨越快，松手后还会再往上冲一点才降下来，要提前松手。鱼用力挣扎时要松一点，张力到 100% 线就断；但线太松鱼会很快游走。
   甩得越远越难控制：张力条的**红色危险区**更长（绿区从 75% 开始、黄区 66%、红区 58%），惯性更大，松线时捕获进度条掉得也更快（黄区 1.35 倍、红区 1.7 倍）。

| 稀有度 | 例子 | 树果 | 经验 | 想跟你走的几率 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 常见 | 鲤鱼王、角金鱼、蚊香蝌蚪… | 0 | 2 | 2% |
| 少见 | 墨海马、大钳蟹、海星星… | 1 | 3 | 3% |
| 稀有 | 灯笼鱼、太阳珊瑚、丑丑鱼… | 3 | 5 | 5% |
| 非常稀有 | 迷你龙、拉普拉斯、暴鲤龙 | 6 | 8 | 8% |

- 共 **24 种**水系宝可梦，每条大小随机（标准身高的 70%–140%），**1/4096** 闪光。甩到红区时非常稀有的只占约 5%。
- 第一次钓到某种额外 **+2 🍓**。所有记录在 📖 图鉴 → 钓鱼图鉴（次数、最大尺寸）。
- **已经钓到过的种类更容易再上钩**：同一稀有度里，已在图鉴中的种类上钩概率是新种类的 **5 倍**，所以越往后越难集齐全图鉴。
- 钓到的宝可梦有时**想跟你走**：选「欢迎加入！」它就成为新伙伴。
- **次数限制**：每天最多上钩 **20 次**，任意 1 小时内最多 **6 次**（有东西上钩就算一次，钓没钓上来都算）。次数用完时不能甩竿，会提示还要等几分钟或明天再来。
- **池塘会累**：1 小时内的前 2 次上钩一定是宝可梦；之后每多一次，钓上**破靴子、空罐头、湿破布、水草、空瓶子**这类垃圾的几率就多 20%（第 6 次时 80%），而且每次上钩都要多等 2.5 秒。垃圾没有任何奖励，也不算每日任务。所以最划算的是每小时钓两三次。

## English

Right-click → "🎣 Fishing…", or 🧭 Outing → "Go fishing" (it shows how many bites are left today and this hour).

1. **Cast**: hold the mouse (or Space) to charge, release to cast. The meter speeds up as it rises and only touches
   the top for a moment, so a full cast takes good timing. The colour sets the difficulty: **green** (close, easy),
   **yellow** (normal), **red** (far, hard, rarer fish).
2. **Wait**: when the bobber dips, **click quickly**. Reaction time is 1.1 s for common fish, only 0.65 s for very rare
   ones; shorter in the red zone, longer in the green one.
3. **Reel in**: hold to reel. The line has **momentum**: holding speeds the tension up, and after you let go it keeps
   rising a little before it drops, so let go early. Ease off when the fish pulls hard – at 100% the line snaps – but
   don't let the line go slack or it swims away fast.
   The farther the cast, the harder it is: the **red danger part** of the tension bar is longer (from 75% in the green
   zone, 66% yellow, 58% red), the line slides more, and a slack line loses the catch bar faster (×1.35 yellow, ×1.7 red).

| Rarity | Examples | Berries | XP | Wants to join |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | Magikarp, Goldeen, Poliwag… | 0 | 2 | 2% |
| Uncommon | Horsea, Krabby, Staryu… | 1 | 3 | 3% |
| Rare | Chinchou, Corsola, Feebas… | 3 | 5 | 5% |
| Very rare | Dratini, Lapras, Gyarados | 6 | 8 | 8% |

- **24** water Pokémon, each with a random size (70%–140% of the usual height), shiny **1 in 4096**. Even in the red zone only about 5% of bites are very rare.
- A new species gives **+2 🍓** extra. Everything goes into 📖 Dex → Fishing Pokédex (count, biggest size).
- **Species you've already caught bite more often**: within a rarity, a species already in the Pokédex is **5 times** as
  likely as a new one, so completing the Pokédex takes a while.
- A catch may **want to come along**: "Welcome to the team!" makes it a new team member.
- **Limits**: at most **20 bites a day** and **6 within any hour** (every bite counts, landed or not). When they're
  used up you can't cast, and it tells you how many minutes to wait (or to come back tomorrow).
- **A tired pond**: the first 2 bites within an hour are always Pokémon; after that each bite is 20% more likely to be
  **junk** – an old boot, an empty can, a soggy rag, seaweed, an empty bottle (80% by the 6th) – and each one takes
  2.5 s longer to come. Junk gives nothing and doesn't count for daily tasks, so a couple of casts an hour is best.
