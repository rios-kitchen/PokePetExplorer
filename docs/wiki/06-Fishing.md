# 钓鱼 🎣 · Fishing 🎣

## 中文

右键 →「🎣 钓鱼…」，或 🧭 外出页 →「去钓鱼」（旁边会显示今天和这一小时还能钓几次）。

**钓鱼地点和原作一样**：每次打开钓鱼窗口，水池就是原作里一个真实的钓鱼地点（数据来自 PokeAPI，按搭档的等级挑选，窗口下方会显示地点，例如「关都 · 12号道路」）。**水池的圈就是钓竿**：浅水 = 破旧钓竿，黄圈 = 好钓竿，红圈 = 厉害钓竿，正中央 = 厉害钓竿且更容易钓到那里稀有的宝可梦。钓到什么、几率多少，都按那个地点那根钓竿的原作出现表；稀有度也按它在那里有多少见来算。伽勒尔和帕底亚还没有钓鱼数据，不会抽到那里。

1. **甩竿**：按住鼠标（或空格）蓄力，松开甩出。水池分成**三圈同心圆**，颜色越深越靠近中心，**浮漂落在哪一圈就是哪个难度**：
   - **浅水区**（三圈以外，靠近码头或池子另一边，绿色）：轻松
   - **黄圈**：普通
   - **红圈**：困难
   - **正中央最深处**（紫色，最小的一圈）：最难，也最容易钓到稀有的宝可梦
   蓄力条从下到上依次是：浅水 → 黄 → 红 → **正中央** → 红 → 黄 → 浅水（甩太远会越过中心），蓄力条上用颜色标出了每一段。蓄力条越往上涨得越快，正中央只停留约 0.13 秒，要掐准时机。
   钓鱼时宝可梦在水池边陪你，桌面上的它会安静待着：不走动、不睡觉、不拉便便，也不会喊饿。
2. **等待**：浮漂往下沉时**尽快点击**。反应时间：普通 1.1 秒，非常稀有的只有 0.65 秒；越靠近中心越短（黄圈 ×0.8、红圈 ×0.7、正中央 ×0.6）。
3. **收线**：按住收线。鱼线有**惯性**：按住时张力会越涨越快，松手后还会再往上冲一点才降下来，要提前松手。鱼用力挣扎时要松一点，张力到 100% 线就断；但线太松鱼会很快游走。
   越靠近中心越难控制：鱼的拉力更大，张力条的**红色危险区**更长（浅水从 66% 开始、黄圈 58%、红圈 52%、正中央 **46%，也就是一半以上都是红色**），惯性更大，松线时捕获进度条掉得也更快。

| 稀有度 | 例子 | 树果 | 经验 | 想跟你走的几率 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 常见 | 鲤鱼王、角金鱼、蚊香蝌蚪… | 0 | 2 | 2% |
| 少见 | 墨海马、大钳蟹、海星星… | 1 | 3 | 3% |
| 稀有 | 灯笼鱼、太阳珊瑚、丑丑鱼… | 3 | 5 | 5% |
| 非常稀有 | 迷你龙、拉普拉斯、暴鲤龙 | 6 | 8 | 8% |

- 能钓到的宝可梦按原作的钓鱼出现表，一共 **58 种**（原来的 24 种加上原作里其他能钓到的），每条大小随机（标准身高的 70%–140%），**1/4096** 闪光。稀有度按它在那个地点那根钓竿里的出现率算（≥40% 常见、≥20% 少见、≥8% 稀有、更少是非常稀有）；正中央会让稀有的更容易上钩。上面表里的奖励和「想跟你走」的几率按这个稀有度。
- 第一次钓到某种额外 **+2 🍓**。所有记录在 📖 图鉴 → 钓鱼图鉴（次数、最大尺寸）。
- **已经钓到过的种类更容易再上钩**：同一稀有度里，已在图鉴中的种类上钩概率是新种类的 **5 倍**，所以越往后越难集齐全图鉴。
- 钓到的宝可梦有时**想跟你走**：选「欢迎加入！」它就成为新伙伴。
- **次数限制**：每天最多上钩 **20 次**，任意 1 小时内最多 **6 次**（有东西上钩就算一次，钓没钓上来都算）。次数用完时不能甩竿，会提示还要等几分钟或明天再来。
- **池塘会累**：1 小时内的前 2 次上钩一定是宝可梦；之后每多一次，钓上**破靴子、空罐头、湿破布、水草、空瓶子**这类垃圾的几率就多 20%（第 6 次时 80%），而且每次上钩都要多等 2.5 秒。垃圾没有任何奖励，也不算每日任务。所以最划算的是每小时钓两三次。

## English

Right-click → "🎣 Fishing…", or 🧭 Outing → "Go fishing" (it shows how many bites are left today and this hour).

**Fishing spots as in the games**: each time you open the fishing window, the pond is a real fishing spot from the games
(PokeAPI data, suited to your partner's level, its name shown below the pond – e.g. "Kanto · Route 12"). **The ring is
the rod**: shallow water = Old Rod, yellow = Good Rod, red = Super Rod, the middle = Super Rod with that spot's rarer
Pokémon likelier. What bites and how often follows that spot's original table for that rod, and rarity is how rare it is
there. Galar and Paldea have no fishing data yet, so they don't come up.

1. **Cast**: hold the mouse (or Space) to charge, release to cast. The pond is **three rings** – darker towards the
   middle – and **the ring the bobber lands in is the difficulty**:
   - **Shallow water** (outside the rings, by the pier or on the far side, green): easy
   - **Yellow ring**: normal
   - **Red ring**: hard
   - **The deep middle** (purple, the smallest ring): hardest, and the best chance of rare Pokémon
   From bottom to top the meter goes shallow → yellow → red → **middle** → red → yellow → shallow (cast too hard and
   you overshoot the middle); the meter shows each band in its colour. It speeds up as it rises and only stays in the
   middle for about 0.13 s, so it takes timing.
   While you fish, your Pokémon is with you at the pond, so on the desktop it stays put: no walking, napping, pooping
   or calling for food.
2. **Wait**: when the bobber dips, **click quickly**. Reaction time is 1.1 s for common fish, only 0.65 s for very rare
   ones; shorter towards the middle (yellow ×0.8, red ×0.7, the middle ×0.6).
3. **Reel in**: hold to reel. The line has **momentum**: holding speeds the tension up, and after you let go it keeps
   rising a little before it drops, so let go early. Ease off when the fish pulls hard – at 100% the line snaps – but
   don't let the line go slack or it swims away fast.
   The nearer the middle, the harder it is: the fish pull harder, the **red danger part** of the tension bar is longer
   (from 66% in shallow water, 58% yellow, 52% red, and **46% in the middle – more than half of it red**), the line
   slides more, and a slack line loses the catch bar faster.

| Rarity | Examples | Berries | XP | Wants to join |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | Magikarp, Goldeen, Poliwag… | 0 | 2 | 2% |
| Uncommon | Horsea, Krabby, Staryu… | 1 | 3 | 3% |
| Rare | Chinchou, Corsola, Feebas… | 3 | 5 | 5% |
| Very rare | Dratini, Lapras, Gyarados | 6 | 8 | 8% |

- **58** Pokémon – the original 24 plus everything else the games let you fish, each with a random size (70%–140% of the usual height), shiny **1 in 4096**. Rarity is how often it bites with that rod at that spot (40%+ common, 20%+ uncommon, 8%+ rare, less very rare); the middle makes the rarer ones likelier. Rewards and the chance it wants to join (table above) follow that rarity.
- A new species gives **+2 🍓** extra. Everything goes into 📖 Dex → Fishing Pokédex (count, biggest size).
- **Species you've already caught bite more often**: within a rarity, a species already in the Pokédex is **5 times** as
  likely as a new one, so completing the Pokédex takes a while.
- A catch may **want to come along**: "Welcome to the team!" makes it a new team member.
- **Limits**: at most **20 bites a day** and **6 within any hour** (every bite counts, landed or not). When they're
  used up you can't cast, and it tells you how many minutes to wait (or to come back tomorrow).
- **A tired pond**: the first 2 bites within an hour are always Pokémon; after that each bite is 20% more likely to be
  **junk** – an old boot, an empty can, a soggy rag, seaweed, an empty bottle (80% by the 6th) – and each one takes
  2.5 s longer to come. Junk gives nothing and doesn't count for daily tasks, so a couple of casts an hour is best.
